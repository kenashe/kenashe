TL;DR: ChatGPT Ads is becoming a real buying-channel experiment, but operators should treat it as a test surface until OpenAI shows more on formats, targeting, reporting, and the line between paid and organic answers.

## What did OpenAI actually announce?

The primary source here is OpenAI’s own post, “ChatGPT Ads expands to 26 new countries as over 100,000 advertisers have used the platform.” OpenAI says more advertisers can now reach people “as they explore, compare and decide what to buy in ChatGPT.”

That sentence matters. OpenAI is not framing ads as a banner unit off to the side. It is framing them around the decision process itself: research, comparison, purchase intent. That is much closer to search marketing than display advertising, but with a different interface. Instead of typing a keyword, clicking ten blue links, and scanning landing pages, a person may ask ChatGPT to narrow options, explain tradeoffs, compare vendors, or recommend what to do next.

The number, over 100,000 advertisers, is also worth reading carefully. OpenAI says advertisers have “used the platform.” That does not mean 100,000 active advertisers today. It does not tell us spend, retention, return on ad spend, category mix, or conversion rates. Useful signal, not proof of a mature ad market.

## Is this search advertising, or something else?

It is search-like, but not identical.

Traditional search ads sit next to a query. ChatGPT Ads can sit inside a longer buying conversation. That changes the job of the ad. A user might not be asking “best CRM for small business” once. They might be asking five follow-ups about budget, integrations, setup time, and whether a cheaper tool is good enough.

That creates a new kind of commercial surface: conversational intent. The question for advertisers is not only “what keyword did they type?” It is “what problem are they trying to solve, and where are they in the decision?”

![shopper moving from broad exploration through comparison into a purchase decision, with a paid placement entering near t](../../assets/blog/2026-10-21-chatgpt-ads-moves-deeper-into-the-buying-journey/inline-1.webp)

I run [Lucky Domains, which works on SEO and search visibility for sites that need to be found](https://luckydomains.io/services.html#seo), and this is the part I’m watching most closely: paid placement inside an answer surface changes what “being discoverable” means. If users make decisions inside ChatGPT, ranking on Google is still valuable, but it may not be the whole visibility game.

The catch is that OpenAI’s announcement, at least from the material provided, does not answer the hard operator questions. What ad formats are available? How is targeting handled? What controls exist for brand safety? How are ads labeled? What reporting do advertisers get? How much of the experience is auction-driven versus curated? Those details decide whether this is a channel teams can manage, or just another black box with nice reach.

## What should marketers test first?

I would not start with a huge budget. I would start with categories where the buyer already needs explanation: software, local services, education, financial workflows, travel planning, home services, B2B tools. Places where a user naturally asks for help comparing options.

The creative should probably look less like a search ad and more like a useful answer fragment. Clear offer. Clear constraint. Clear proof. If your product is only good for a narrow customer, say that. Conversational systems punish vague positioning because the user keeps asking follow-ups.

The measurement plan matters too. Do not only track last-click conversions. Watch assisted conversions, branded search lift, direct traffic, demo quality, and post-click behavior. A ChatGPT-originated buyer may arrive warmer because the comparison already happened upstream.

For a builder or operator, I’d run this as a controlled experiment: pick one product line, one geography now covered by OpenAI, one landing page built for high-intent comparison traffic, and one clean conversion event. The miss most teams will make is treating ChatGPT Ads like another keyword channel. The better test is whether your offer survives an AI-mediated comparison, because that is where the buying journey is moving.
